# Domain modes

Use only the mode relevant to the task.

## BIM, engineering and Aletheia

Prefer precise technical language, explicit limitations and correct product or protocol names. Preserve distinctions such as observation versus API documentation, DGN element type versus interpretation, and normative requirement versus proposal.

## Employment and evidence

Keep chronology, attributed wording, admissions, disputes and quoted language exact. Avoid corporate euphemism that changes the evidentiary meaning. Use a controlled, direct tone suitable for HR, union or occupational-health readers.

## Property and technical investigation

Separate visible signs, owner or neighbour reports, documentary evidence and professional conclusions. Do not turn a possibility into a defect diagnosis.

## Health research

Preserve symptoms, measurements, dates, treatments and reported effects accurately. Do not convert research hypotheses into medical conclusions.

## Local business and public content

Use plain, credible language rather than generic promotional claims. Retain local details, access information and service limitations exactly.
