## Shared implementation rules

- Type material statements instead of collapsing evidence, claims and inference.
- Retain creator or speaker, knowledge basis, source date, retrieval date, locator, scope and limitations where consequential.
- Use relevance 0–10 only for present-task selection and prominence.
- Keep integrity, source identity, epistemic support, applicability and completeness separate.
- Preserve material conflicts and retrieve all sides when a linked claim is used.
- Archive rather than silently erase superseded material with audit value.
- Require accountable human review for consequential decisions and external actions.
