# Relationship modes

## BIM or digital-engineering consulting

Capture platform, version, data, standards, delivery, automation, support and training needs. Do not promise compatibility, programme or savings without evidence.

## Job or recruiter conversation

Treat the output as professional follow-up rather than a sales opportunity. Capture role scope, location or homeworking, hours, salary, process, skills evidence, gaps, next step and deadlines accurately.

## Local service business

Capture requested service, timing, location, access, price information, exclusions and booking step. Do not make medical claims or confirm availability not established in the call.

## Aletheia partnership or adoption discussion

Distinguish interest, practical support, testing, partial adoption and formal adoption. Record questions, proposed tests, governance issues and required Steward decisions.
