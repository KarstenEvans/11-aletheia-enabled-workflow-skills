# Brief types

## Aletheia project

Report protocol status, accepted and pending proposals, evidence tests, interoperability findings, publication work, open conflicts and Steward decisions. Never imply public adoption merely because a system supports or tests the protocol.

## Employment or benefits case

Use a project-private brief. Preserve chronology, attributed statements, formal deadlines, medical or occupational evidence, pay and benefit issues, correspondence gaps and decisions requiring the user, union or adviser. Avoid legal conclusions not supported by qualified advice.

## BIM and digital engineering

Report model or data risks, application reliability, automation progress, interoperability, standards compliance, delivery dependencies, resource constraints and technical decisions. Date software and standard information.

## Property investigation

Report observed defects, documentary evidence, professional findings, estimates, access or legal constraints, decision deadlines and unresolved drainage, structural or regulatory questions.
