# Domain modes

## Employment, HR, union and occupational health

Treat meeting dates, allegations, admissions, policy statements, deadlines, pay or benefit references and requests for action as evidence-sensitive. Preserve the sender's exact position and distinguish it from the user's proposed response. Surface contradictions across threads.

## BIM and technical work

Prioritise requests with delivery dates, model or data risks, version dependencies, safety implications or blocked collaborators. Do not infer a technical commitment that the user did not make.

## Property and contractors

Surface appointments, quotes, access requirements, planning or Building Control issues, warranties and payment stages. Keep quotation scope separate from technical diagnosis.

## Local business

Distinguish customer enquiries, bookings, complaints, supplier requests and marketing mail. Never expose one customer's information in another draft.
