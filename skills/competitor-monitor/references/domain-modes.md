# Monitoring profiles

## Bentley, BIM and engineering applications

Track official release notes, documentation, deprecation notices, API or SDK changes, licensing, interoperability, platform support and strategically relevant recruitment. Separate vendor claims from tested behaviour and community reports.

## IFC, openBIM and ISO 19650

Track buildingSMART and standards-body publications, implementation guidance, certification and material vendor support. Date editions and distinguish draft, published and superseded standards.

## Aletheia and AI interoperability

Track comparable protocols, agent-memory systems, provenance methods, adoption claims, repositories, licences, governance changes and public tests. Distinguish marketing similarity from genuine protocol overlap.

## Local service businesses

Use only public business information. Track material service, pricing, booking, location, review-pattern and search-positioning changes. Do not collect unnecessary personal information about staff or customers.
