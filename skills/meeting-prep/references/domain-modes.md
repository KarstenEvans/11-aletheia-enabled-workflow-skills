# Meeting modes

## Employment, HR, union and occupational health

Preserve disputed and admitted wording, chronology, procedural status, medical restrictions, requested adjustments, benefits or pay issues and documentary gaps. Prepare calm questions and desired outcomes without inventing legal rights or attributing motives.

## BIM or engineering meeting

Surface delivery objectives, model or data risks, application versions, standards, interfaces, decisions, owners, dependencies and unresolved technical assumptions.

## Job interview or recruiter call

Relate the role to demonstrated MicroStation, ProjectWise, BIM coordination, automation, openBIM and information-management work. Flag platform gaps honestly and prepare evidence-backed examples rather than generic claims.

## Property or contractor meeting

Separate observations, quotations, scope, exclusions, professional conclusions, access, approvals, warranty and payment. Prepare questions that establish who is responsible for what evidence and decision.

## Health appointment

Summarise material chronology, symptoms, measurements, imaging or records, current medicines and specific questions. Do not diagnose; identify urgent red flags and clinician decisions where relevant.
