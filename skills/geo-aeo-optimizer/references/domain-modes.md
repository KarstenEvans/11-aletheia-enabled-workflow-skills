# Domain modes

## BIM and technical authority

Prefer official Bentley, buildingSMART, ISO or government documentation, version dates, reproducible examples and explicit product names. Do not overstate support for an API or standard based on forum discussion alone.

## Aletheia protocol

Make definitions, conformance levels, normative hierarchy and adoption status directly answerable. Link claims to the authoritative specification and do not present a case study or proposal as normative protocol.

## Local service business

Prioritise service scope, location, opening or booking information, access and parking, pricing policy, practitioner qualifications, FAQs and genuine local distinctions. Avoid unsubstantiated health or superiority claims.

## Evidence-led consumer or property research

Lead with the bounded conclusion, date it, explain the source class and preserve important uncertainty. Do not optimise away warnings or conflicts merely because they reduce promotional force.
