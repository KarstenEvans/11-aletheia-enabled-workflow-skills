# Meeting Prep

Turn scattered context into a briefing readable in under five minutes.

## Resolve the meeting and sources

Determine the event, attendees, roles, organisation, purpose and time. Verify ambiguous people rather than assuming that similar names refer to the same person.

Ask where authorised calendar information, notes, CRM records, call summaries, email threads, proposals and account records live. If access is unavailable, request the relevant material. Use current external research only when permitted and cite it.

## Assemble the history

Review previous interactions, promises, open opportunities, proposals, blockers, disputed points and overdue follow-ups. Keep the other party's statements distinct from the user's account or inference.

## Apply enhanced Aletheia controls

Create stable IDs for attendees, commitments, questions, proposals, constraints and current company claims. Retain author or speaker, role, knowledge basis, source-created or observed date, retrieval date, locator, claim scope, limitations and privacy class.

Score `relevance: 0–10` for the meeting and explain scores of 7 or above. Relevance-0 corrected spelling or exact duplication should not clutter the brief unless identity remains uncertain or an audit trail is necessary.

Detect contradictions in identity, dates, promises, prices, responsibilities, prior accounts and current external information. Preserve all material sides in a conflict record and do not choose a convenient version without support.

Report source integrity, identity, epistemic support, present applicability and completeness separately. Unknown identity or missing history remains explicit.

Give inferred priorities, objections, sensitivities and recommended angles an interpretation receipt containing evidence used, inference, alternative explanation, applicability, uncertainty and consequence if wrong.

Date current external information and flag anything that should be rechecked immediately before the meeting.

## Output

1. Meeting snapshot.
2. Relevant history and commitments.
3. Current organisation context.
4. Likely priorities, clearly marked as stated or inferred.
5. Recommended talking points.
6. Strategic questions.
7. Possible objections, conflicts, risks and sensitivities.
8. Provenance trace and human-review checklist.

Do not contact attendees, alter the calendar or expose unrelated private information. Read [references/domain-modes.md](references/domain-modes.md) for meeting-specific guidance.
