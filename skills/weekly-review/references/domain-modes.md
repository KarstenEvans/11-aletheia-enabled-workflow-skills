# Review streams

Use only streams relevant to the week.

## Professional and BIM

Track deliverables, blocked dependencies, application or data risks, automation improvements, learning, job applications and consequential communications.

## Aletheia

Track accepted decisions, open proposals, test results, conflicts, interoperability feedback, publication work and next Steward-review points.

## Employment case management

Track deadlines, documents received, emails awaiting response, union or insurer contact, occupational-health evidence and unresolved contradictions. Keep this stream project-private.

## Property and maintenance

Track inspections, moisture or structural observations, quotes, professional appointments, seasonal work and safety-critical items.

## Health administration

Track appointments, records requested, material measurements and questions for clinicians. Do not turn the weekly review into diagnosis or treatment direction.

## Local business and content

Track enquiries, bookings, customer issues, content published, search visibility, affiliate administration and high-value follow-ups.
