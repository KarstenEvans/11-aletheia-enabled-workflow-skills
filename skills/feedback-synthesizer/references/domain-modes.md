# Feedback contexts

## Aletheia interoperability tests

Separate protocol strengths, usability problems, non-conformance, interpretation differences and model-specific limitations. Preserve each AI's adoption status and interpretation receipts. Treat successful formatting as different from successful epistemic discipline.

## BIM and technical users

Separate software defects, configuration problems, version limitations, training gaps, data-quality issues and workflow preferences. Retain product versions and file or dataset context.

## Website and local service customers

Distinguish booking, location, parking, price, service, trust, accessibility and search-discovery feedback. Do not infer medical outcomes or identify customers unnecessarily.

## Employment or occupational processes

Use only when authorised and project-private. Separate individual testimony, repeated procedural patterns, policy evidence and interpretation. Do not generalise from one person's case.

## Health or treatment experience

Treat anecdotes and self-reports as testimony, not clinical efficacy evidence. Record dates, context and limitations and identify professional review where conclusions could affect care.
