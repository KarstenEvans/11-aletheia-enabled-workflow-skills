# Sales Followup

Advance the conversation without exaggerating interest, fit or commitments.

## Learn the style

When necessary, request representative follow-up emails, CRM notes and proposal summaries. Use the user's Humanizer profile when available.

## Analyse the call

Extract the other party's stated goals, context, problems, priorities, objections, decision criteria, timing, budget signals, stakeholders, commitments and buying or engagement signals.

Anchor material findings to transcript timestamps or note locations and retain the call date, participant identity status and retrieval date. Keep explicit statements separate from interpretation.

Assign proposed actions `relevance: 0–10` based on their connection to stated goals and agreed next steps. Relevance is not truth confidence.

Create a conflict record for incompatible commitments, prices, deadlines, responsibilities or stakeholder accounts. Label buying intent, implied needs and fit as interpretation unless stated directly. For consequential inferences, provide an interpretation receipt with evidence, alternative explanation, applicability and confidence reasons.

Flag uncertain names, poor transcription and qualification gaps.

## Produce four outputs

1. **Follow-up email:** brief, specific, non-pushy and confirming the actual discussion and next step.
2. **CRM-style note:** factual and scannable, covering goals, objections, stakeholders, signals, risks and next actions.
3. **Action checklist:** responsibilities separated by party, with dates and dependencies where stated.
4. **Proposal guidance:** only when need, fit and intent are sufficiently supported; otherwise state what qualification is missing.

Never send the email or update a CRM. Present every draft for approval. Read [references/domain-modes.md](references/domain-modes.md) for the relevant relationship type.
