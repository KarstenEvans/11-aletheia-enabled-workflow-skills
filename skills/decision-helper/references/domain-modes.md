# Domain modes

## Employment and benefits

Separate contractual text, employer statements, insurer or union information, medical restrictions and user interpretation. Preserve dates and conflicting accounts. Do not recommend resignation, formal accusations or irreversible escalation without considering role, income, benefits, evidence and professional advice.

## Health

Treat symptoms, measurements, imaging, medical notes, treatment response and mechanistic hypotheses as different node types. Use current authoritative medical sources and identify clinician review points. Do not infer diagnosis or causation from temporal association alone.

## Property

Separate visible defects, documentary records, neighbour reports, estimates and professional conclusions. Include planning, Building Regulations, drainage, structural, tenure, insurance, finance and resale constraints where relevant.

## BIM and technical architecture

Account for platform support, file integrity, interoperability, version lock-in, automation maintenance, security, training effort and reversibility. Prefer official documentation for current capabilities.

## Aletheia

Distinguish normative specification changes from examples or implementation experiments. Record proposed changes rather than silently rewriting accepted protocol knowledge.
