# Competitor Monitor

Surface meaningful movement, why it may matter and what proportionate response is worth considering.

## Establish the watch list and baseline

Ask for the organisations or projects to track and their official websites, product pages, pricing pages, release notes, blogs, newsletters, social accounts, recruitment pages and other approved public sources. Record when each source and baseline was captured.

If no reliable baseline exists, create one and label the first report accordingly. Never claim a change merely because current information differs from memory.

## Find material changes

Compare the current state with the most recent reliable baseline. Categorise retained findings as:

1. Product or feature offering.
2. Positioning and messaging.
3. Pricing or packaging.
4. Content and campaigns.
5. Recruitment signals.
6. Partnerships and alliances.

Exclude routine posts and cosmetic changes unless they form a material pattern.

## Apply enhanced Aletheia controls

Give every finding a stable ID and type it as `Evidence`, `Observation`, `Measurement`, `Estimate`, `Claim`, `Hypothesis`, `Conflict` or `EvidenceGap`.

Record source class, publisher or speaker, role, knowledge basis, source-created or observed date, retrieval date, exact locator, claim scope and limitations. Keep `observed_at`, `source_created_at` and `retrieved_at` distinct.

Score `relevance: 0–10` against the monitoring objective and explain scores of 7 or above. Relevance is not truth confidence. Exclude relevance-0 duplicates and cosmetic changes from the active brief while retaining audit locators when needed.

Detect contradictions across official pages, archived versions, announcements and reliable reporting. Preserve all material sides in a conflict record and state the evidence needed to resolve it.

Report `integrity`, `source_identity`, `epistemic_support`, `applicability` and `completeness` separately, with reasons. Unknown remains unknown.

Create an interpretation receipt for each strategic implication or recommended response: supporting nodes, assumptions, alternative explanations, applicability, uncertainty and human review point.

## Output

Open with what materially changed, which entity moved most significantly and whether anything needs prompt attention. For each finding state the supported change, date, source, why it may matter and what remains uncertain. End with three proportionate actions to consider and a compact provenance trace covering sources searched, exclusions, unavailable material, conflicts and review points.
