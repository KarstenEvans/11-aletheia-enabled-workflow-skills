# Weekly Review

Create the management picture without burying it in administrative detail.

## Set up the source map

Ask where authorised pipeline or CRM records, revenue and invoices, payments, open tasks, completed work and published material live. Record the approved locations or tell the user exactly what to paste each week. Do not imply a connection that is unavailable.

## Review the period

Use the declared reporting period and produce:

1. **Wins:** specific outcomes completed.
2. **Stalled or slipping:** missed deadlines or items with no meaningful movement for at least two weeks.
3. **Money:** invoiced, received and outstanding amounts with dates when available.
4. **Pipeline:** leads and stages, plus the single follow-up most likely to matter.
5. **Next three priorities:** ranked by expected impact, with one-sentence reasons.
6. **Candid observation:** one evidence-based pattern the user may be overlooking, stated without psychological diagnosis.

## Apply the Aletheia layer

Record source creation and retrieval dates and a locator or system record ID. Score candidate items `relevance: 0–10` for this review. Omit relevance-0 duplicates and corrected noise from the brief while retaining an audit reference when necessary.

Preserve conflicting figures, statuses or owners in a conflict entry. Report separate confidence dimensions for source integrity, identity, epistemic support, current applicability and completeness. Mark stale or missing records rather than filling gaps.

Keep the result to roughly one page and actionable in about 15 minutes. A scheduled run may prepare the review, but it must not update CRM, tasks, financial records or messages without approval.
