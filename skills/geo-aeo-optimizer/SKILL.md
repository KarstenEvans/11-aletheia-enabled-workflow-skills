# Geo Aeo Optimizer

Improve extractability and citation fitness without cosmetically strengthening unsupported claims.

## Pass 1: audit

Assess whether:

- The core question receives a direct answer near the beginning.
- Important claims are precise, self-contained and supportable.
- Relevant numbers, dates, named entities and methods are present.
- Authorship and genuine expertise are stated clearly.
- Headings, definitions, paragraphs, lists and tables are easy to parse.
- Material claims use appropriate primary or authoritative sources.

Show gaps, unsupported claims and ambiguous wording. Confident phrasing is not evidence.

## Apply the Aletheia evidence layer

For every material claim retain source creator, source-created date, retrieval date, locator, support scope and limitations. Assign `relevance: 0–10` to the target question, not perceived truth.

Detect contradictions between the page and its sources or among cited sources. Preserve material sides. Report epistemic support, applicability and completeness separately; do not convert search rank, link count or semantic similarity into confidence.

## Pass 2: revise

Lead with a direct answer, improve headings, make supported claims precise and make key passages understandable outside their surrounding paragraph. Add a concise FAQ based on realistic user questions.

Retain the user's voice. Do not fabricate expertise, credentials, data or citations. Flag claims requiring research rather than making them sound stronger.

Return the audit before the revised version. Read [references/domain-modes.md](references/domain-modes.md) for domain-specific optimisation.
