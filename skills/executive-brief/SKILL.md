# Executive Brief

Report what changed, what is blocked, what is at risk and where accountable decisions are needed.

## Resolve the evidence set

Ask where authorised updates, project records, dashboards, metrics, meeting notes and strategic documents are stored. Use the supplied or connected sources; do not imply access to unavailable systems.

Identify only developments with material business, project or case impact. Connect related information across sources where support exists, but do not reproduce every status update.

## Apply enhanced Aletheia controls

Assign a stable ID and epistemic type to every item capable of changing a decision. Retain source owner or author, role, knowledge basis, reporting period, source-created date, retrieval date, locator, claim scope, limitations and privacy class.

Score `relevance: 0–10` for this brief and explain scores of 7 or above. Mandatory constraints, major conflicts and decision deadlines must still be surfaced. Relevance is not confidence.

Detect conflicting metrics, accounts, owners, deadlines and causal explanations. Create one conflict record linking all material sides; state whether it is unresolved, partially resolved or resolved and what evidence or authorised decision is required.

Report separately, with reasons:

- `integrity`
- `source_identity`
- `epistemic_support`
- `applicability`
- `completeness`

Date every current-state conclusion and flag expired, stale or future-effective information. Record a review date for volatile claims.

Give every consequential recommendation an interpretation receipt containing supporting evidence, assumptions, credible alternative interpretations, known conflicts, applicability limits and accountable human review.

## Output

Use this structure:

1. Executive summary.
2. Key wins.
3. Risks and deteriorating indicators.
4. Blocked work, owners and dependencies.
5. Decisions needed, decision owner, deadline and cost of delay.
6. Recommended priorities.
7. Conflicts and evidence gaps requiring attention.
8. Compact provenance trace and Steward-review points.

Keep fact, analysis, recommendation and authorised decision visibly distinct. Do not distribute the brief or update source systems without approval.
