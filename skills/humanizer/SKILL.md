# Humanizer

Write in the user's evidenced voice without inventing biography, experience, emotion or facts.

## Set up or refresh the voice profile

1. Ask for three to five substantial samples of the user's genuine writing when no adequate profile exists.
2. Record each sample's creator, approximate date, audience and purpose when known.
3. Analyse sentence rhythm, paragraphing, vocabulary, directness, formality, humour, openings, closings and characteristic phrases.
4. Give greater relevance to samples matching the present audience and current period. Do not invisibly blend materially different historical or audience-specific voices.
5. Treat exact duplicates, corrected spelling and formatting noise as relevance 0 unless identity or meaning is affected.

## Choose the mode

- **Generation:** write new material using the profile and the requested purpose, audience and format.
- **Editing:** preserve the draft's meaning and factual content while removing empty introductions, needless hedging, inflated corporate wording, repetitive rhetorical contrasts, excessive em dashes and expressions unsupported by the user's samples.

If the user requests a different tone, retain the underlying voice and make only the necessary adjustment.

## Preserve evidentiary meaning

For employment, health, property, legal-adjacent or other evidence-sensitive material:

- Preserve the difference between what the user admitted, disputed, observed, inferred or was told.
- Do not soften or intensify quotations, dates, names, allegations or commitments.
- Corrected name spellings may disappear from the active rendition when relevance is 0, but never allow a correction to merge two uncertain identities.
- Flag contradictions that affect meaning instead of editing them away.

## Output

Return clean, ready-to-use text unless the user requests tracked changes, rationale or alternatives. Do not add facts, quotations, sources or personal experiences that were not supplied.

Read [references/domain-modes.md](references/domain-modes.md) when the writing concerns one of the user's recurring work domains.
